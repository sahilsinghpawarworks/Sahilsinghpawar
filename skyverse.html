Made for Skyblue — Your creative place

Role in project is UI/UX design intern

Team is 4 designers

Date is April 2026

(Details)

An in-store AR system for Skyblue that reads a shopper's mood, wraps them in a matching colour aura and recommends stationery to fit, turning a crowded aisle into a personal, playful moment.

Platform: In-store AR screen

Tools: Figma, Stable Diffusion, TouchDesigner

(A closer look)

(01)Context

Made during my UI/UX internship at Skyblue in 2026, with Prachi Sandal, Simran Kaur Somal and Pritam Kumar Tudu. The brief: design and pilot a multisensory, cognitive AR intervention for customer engagement at the Skyblue Experience Centre.

(02)The problem

Skyblue offers a wide range of stationery but lacks interactive personalisation, emotional engagement and creative interactivity. Young, creative shoppers feel overwhelmed by choice and struggle to find products that match their mood or intention, so the journey feels disconnected and uninspired.

(03)The process

Research

Grounded in research on AR, multisensory experience and consumer psychology, then a literature review, UTAUT2 and activity-theory analysis, case studies, a survey, user interviews, personas, empathy and affinity mapping, and a Kano model.

Phases

  1. Context & ecosystem understanding
  2. Research foundation
  3. Benchmarking & primary research
  4. Need-gap identification & problem definition
  5. Concept development
  6. Prototype development

(04)The solution

An in-store AR system scans the shopper's facial expression, detects their current emotion and generates a matching aura colour, shown as artistic AR overlays around them. Based on the mood it recommends products: calming blue pens, energising yellow notebooks, focused-toned planners. It cuts choice overload and makes shopping personal, playful and inspiring.
