(Details)

A mobile navigation and service app for Apollo Hospitals, designed so patients and visitors, including blind people, can find their way through a large hospital without stress.

Platform: Mobile app

Tools: Figma, Illustrator, Shapr3D

(A closer look)

(01)Context

A hospital-navigation project on Apollo Hospitals, including a field study at Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad and a comparison with the Mayo Clinic's wayfinding.

(02)The problem

Hospitals like Apollo are large, high-traffic buildings with complex layouts. Patients and visitors, often stressed and unfamiliar with the space, struggle to reach the right department, find emergency exits, or locate services like the pharmacy, especially under time pressure.

(03)The process

Research

Site visits documenting signage, wayfinding and colour-code protocols; user personas and journey mapping; a review of Apollo's website and app; and competitive analysis against other hospital apps. Need gaps we found:

  • Finding reliable information and booking appointments
  • Uncertainty about waiting times
  • Getting lost in a huge hospital
  • Lack of accessible communication and support
  • Unclear costs and insurance
  • Delays in getting medication and no follow-up care

How it came together

The competitive analysis showed Apollo's app lacked appointment scheduling, indoor hospital navigation, queue management and multilingual support, so the app proposal focused there.

(04)The solution

An app with indoor navigation to departments, live queue management and step-by-step wayfinding, paired with accessibility for blind people: audio-guided navigation and tactile pathways from entrances to registration, wards and washrooms.
