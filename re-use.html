Made for Design & Art Colleges

Role in project is [YOUR ROLE]

Team is 8 designers

Date is December 2024

(Details)

A phygital platform where design and art students buy, sell and pass on pre-owned stationery and art supplies, cutting what they spend and what goes to waste.

Platform: App + shared space

Tools: Figma, Illustrator, Procreate

(A closer look)

(01)Context

A design-thinking project for design and art colleges, made with a team of eight. We followed empathise, define, ideate and design.

(02)The problem

Stationery and art supplies are a heavy, recurring cost for design students, and leftover materials rarely find a second user. Students want to pass things on but can't find anyone to take them.

(03)The process

Research

A survey and interviews with students. Key findings:

  • 53.8% had taken leftover stationery or art supplies from seniors
  • 64.1% are interested in reusing seniors' materials
  • 67.9% thought of selling or donating leftovers but couldn't find anyone to take them
  • 67.9% are interested in reselling excess materials to juniors

How it came together

Two personas guided the design: Arjun, a budget-conscious design student, and Rita, a quality-oriented freelance artist. We mapped empathy, information architecture and buy-and-sell user flows, then moved from wireframes to brand and UI.

(04)The solution

An app that makes buying and selling pre-owned stationery simple, safe and community-driven, with a shared stationery workspace alongside it. It promotes affordability, reuse and sustainability through a circular economy among students.
