About

Short bio

Master of Design in UI/UX from NIFT, with a background in footwear design from FDDI. Right now I'm designing for virtual reality at IIT Roorkee.

I specialise in user experience design, research, service design and digital product experiences. Before UX, I worked in footwear and accessory design, including a full-time role as Assistant Accessory Designer at Rajesh Pratap Singh.

Based in Faridabad, Haryana. Available for freelance and full-time work.

What I do

UI/UX design

Intuitive digital experiences that simplify complexity, built around user needs, accessibility and engagement.

Product design

Design thinking, research and innovation that connect user needs with business goals.

Visual design

Aesthetics, storytelling and detail across interfaces, brand assets and print.

Leather & footwear

Design, craftsmanship and material knowledge, balancing function, comfort and style.
