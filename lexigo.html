Made for Crossword

Role in project is [YOUR ROLE]

Team is 4 designers

Date is December 2024

(Details)

A smart book-discovery kiosk and companion app for Crossword bookstores that helps people find books, check availability and browse more easily in store.

Platform: Kiosk + mobile app

Tools: Figma

(A closer look)

(01)Context

An interface-design brief in edutainment: pick a domain, study its physical, digital and phygital interfaces, find the gaps, and design an intervention. Field study at Crossword, Palladium Mall, Ahmedabad.

(02)The problem

How might we improve in-store navigation and give people a mobile-based way to find books, check availability and enjoy browsing at Crossword?

(03)The process

Research

We visited several bookstores and surveyed staff and customers. Gaps at Crossword:

  • Books hard to locate because of layout and limited signage
  • Only one billing PC, causing queues at peak hours
  • No mobile app for availability checks or store navigation
  • Overcrowded display sections and limited reading space

How it came together

We mapped physical, digital and phygital touchpoints, built a customer persona, and designed the key flows for search, availability and ordering.

(04)The solution

Smart Book Discovery Kiosk: touchscreens at the entrance, centre and billing that act as a digital book guide and ordering point, with a companion app. The design uses bold book covers, simple icons (search, heart, cart, QR), swipeable shelves, large text and Crossword's palette.

(05)The impact

  • Shoppers can explore even out-of-stock books
  • Less frustration from unavailability
  • More traffic to the official site
  • Staff spend less time on inventory questions
