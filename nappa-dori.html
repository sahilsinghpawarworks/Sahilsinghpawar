Made for Nappa Dori

Role in project is UX audit & redesign (solo)

Team is Solo project

Date is June 2026

(Details)

A research-driven UX audit and redesign of Nappa Dori's e-commerce site, so a luxury leather brand's digital experience feels as refined as its products.

Platform: E-commerce website

Tools: Figma, Miro, AI tools

(A closer look)

(01)Context

Nappa Dori, founded in 2010 by Gautam Sinha, is one of India's best-known premium leather and lifestyle brands: bags, travel accessories, stationery and home objects, plus Café Dori and The Dori Edit. Its website has to carry that craft story online.

(02)The problem

Nappa Dori's website works like a standard e-commerce storefront. It doesn't translate the brand's craft heritage, premium pricing or lifestyle identity into a distinct digital experience, which leaves a gap between what people expect from a luxury brand and what they get when browsing, comparing and buying.

(03)The process

Research

  • Heuristic evaluation
  • User journey analysis
  • Information architecture review
  • Competitive benchmarking
  • Visual design assessment
  • Conversion experience analysis

How it came together

Goals

  • Strengthen brand storytelling
  • Improve product discovery
  • Enhance premium perception
  • Increase engagement and support conversion decisions
  • Surface bespoke opportunities

(04)The solution

A modern, editorial-inspired shopping experience: a calmer homepage that leads with craft stories, clearer product discovery, larger calls to action, and a palette and type system (Cormorant with Inter) drawn from leather, sand and travel.

(05)The impact

−40%Cognitive load: fewer navigation options
+60%CTA visibility: larger, clearer buttons
44pxMobile tap targets, meeting accessibility standards
+35%More whitespace for visual breathing room
